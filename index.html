Bread sign-in sheet check

Open this page in Bread's in-app browser on a phone. Each button calls window.bread.auth.open(url) and shows what came back, with the time, under the button and in the log at the bottom.

Status

Expected on a phone: keys version, presentation, auth, frozen, version 1, and the property not writable or configurable. In Bread's desktop app: no keys, frozen, no version, presentation or auth, and the property writable and configurable.
